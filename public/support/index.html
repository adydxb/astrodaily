Frequently asked
How do I manage my subscription?+

Manage or cancel your subscription in the Apple App Store or Google Play account used to purchase AstroDaily. Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription.

How do I update my profile details?+

Open Profile in AstroDaily to update your name, birth date, optional birth time and place, profile photo, language, theme, or reminder settings.

Can I restore a Premium purchase?+

Yes. Open Profile or the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the original app-store account.

How do I turn on a daily reminder?+

Open Profile, turn on Daily Horoscope Reminder, allow notifications, and choose your reminder time. If notifications were previously denied, enable them in your device settings.

Why is my widget not up to date?+

Widgets refresh according to your device and operating system. Open AstroDaily to refresh today's horoscope and ratings, then check that the widget is still added to your Home Screen.

How is my horoscope created?+

AstroDaily creates reflective content on your device from your zodiac sign and the selected day. Horoscopes, ratings, compatibility, and forecasts are for entertainment and general reflection.
