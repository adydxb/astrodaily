AstroDaily Data Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with your AstroDaily app usage or subscription records, what can be deleted, and what may need to be retained.

To request deletion, email support@astrodailyapp.com with the subject “AstroDaily Data Deletion Request.”

Effective August 3, 2026

AstroDaily does not require an account

AstroDaily does not require you to create an account, and there is no username or password to sign in with. Your astrology profile — your name, birth date, optional birth time and place, zodiac sign, partner sign, language, theme, and reminder settings — is stored locally on your device rather than in an AstroDaily account.

Because of this, most of your information is under your direct control. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data from that device, subject to your operating system's backup behavior. A request through this page is for records that are not stored on your device, described below.

What you can request deletion of

You can ask us to delete data associated with your app usage or subscription records that is held by us or by a service provider acting on our behalf. This includes subscription and entitlement records used to unlock and restore Premium, and any information you have previously sent to support.

How to submit a request

To request deletion, email us with the subject “AstroDaily Data Deletion Request.”

support@astrodailyapp.com

Please include:

  • Whether you use Android or iOS
  • The email address you want us to reply to
  • A short description of what you want deleted

Do not include payment-card details, passwords, or other sensitive information.

What can be deleted

Where applicable, and subject to the retention limits described below, a request can cover:

  • Your RevenueCat customer record and related app-level subscription metadata
  • Any support information you voluntarily submitted to us, such as your email correspondence and its attachments

Deleting subscription metadata may prevent an existing purchase from being restored in the app. If you hold an active subscription, please read the section on cancelling before you send your request.

What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained after a deletion request. We may retain:

  • Transaction records that we are required to keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes
  • Purchase history that is controlled independently by Google Play or Apple

Purchase records held by Google Play or Apple are controlled by those companies under their own terms and privacy policies. AstroDaily cannot delete them on your behalf. To ask about records held by a store, please contact Google Play or Apple Support directly.

Cancelling a subscription

A deletion request does not cancel a subscription, and cancelling is not something AstroDaily can do for you. An auto-renewing subscription must be cancelled in the account settings of the store used to purchase it — Google Play on Android, or your Apple account settings on iOS.

Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription either. If you want billing to stop, cancel through the store first, then send your deletion request.

How requests are handled

We review each request and process it within a reasonable period. We may need to contact you at the email address you write from to confirm the request or ask for details that help us identify the correct records. If we cannot locate a matching record, or if part of a request falls under the retention categories above, we will tell you what we were able to action.
