Privacy Policy

AstroDaily is designed to keep your personal astrology profile on your device. This policy explains the limited situations in which information is sent to a service provider so a feature can work.

Effective August 1, 2026

Information stored on your device

AstroDaily does not require an account. Your name, birth date, optional birth time and place, calculated zodiac sign, chosen partner sign, language, theme, reminder settings, Premium status cache, and selected profile-photo reference are stored locally on your device. This information is used to personalize the app and its widgets.

Horoscopes and astrology features

Daily horoscopes, themes, ratings, compatibility, calendar guidance, birth-chart details, lucky insights, and widget content are generated on your device from content included with the app. Your profile and horoscope requests are not sent to our servers for generation, and these features can work offline.

Photos and device permissions

If you choose a profile photo, AstroDaily asks for photo-library access in response to that action. The selected photo reference remains on your device and is not uploaded by AstroDaily. You can deny or change photo access in your device settings.

Daily reminders

If you turn on the Daily Horoscope Reminder, AstroDaily requests notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your device at the time you choose. The reminder setting and notification content are not sent to our servers. You can turn reminders off in the app or change notification permission in device settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase or entitlement information needed to display offers, unlock Premium, and restore or manage access. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, as applicable. AstroDaily does not receive or store your payment-card details.

App updates

AstroDaily may contact Expo's update infrastructure to check for and download app updates. An update check may include technical information such as the app and runtime version, platform, release channel, and update identifier. It does not include your name, birth details, zodiac sign, selected photo, or horoscope content.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. Your email provider also processes that message. Please do not send sensitive personal information. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or meet legal obligations.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

AstroDaily currently contains no advertising SDK, does not sell personal information, and does not perform cross-app tracking. We do not currently use an analytics or diagnostics service that sends app-usage analytics or crash diagnostics to us. If these practices change, we will update this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures.

Your choices and data deletion

You can edit your profile, language, theme, partner sign, photo, and reminder settings in the app. Because the profile is stored locally and no AstroDaily account is created, deleting the app removes its on-device app data, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Purchase records remain with the relevant app store and RevenueCat as required to provide and restore subscriptions.

Service providers and security

We use service providers only where needed to operate purchases, app distribution and updates, or support. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

AstroDaily is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date.

Questions about privacy? support@astrodailyapp.com.
