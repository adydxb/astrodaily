Privacy Policy

AstroDaily is designed to keep your personal profile on your device. This policy explains what stays local and what is sent only when the app needs a service to work.

Effective July 31, 2026

What stays on your device

AstroDaily does not require an account. Profile details such as your name, birth date, birth time, and birth location remain on your device and are not sent to our servers for horoscope generation.

How horoscopes are generated

AstroDaily generates your horoscopes entirely on your device from content included in the app. No horoscope request is sent to us or to any third party, and horoscopes work even when you are offline.

Subscriptions and purchases

RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase or entitlement information needed to provide, restore, and manage subscriptions. Purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. Apple may process payment information according to its own privacy policy. AstroDaily does not receive or store your payment card details.

App updates

AstroDaily can check for over-the-air app updates. An update check sends the app and runtime version, your platform, the release channel, and an update identifier. It does not include your name, birth details, zodiac sign, or any other profile data.

Support messages

Support information is transmitted only when you voluntarily choose to contact us and send a message. The website does not send support information in the background.

What we do not do

AstroDaily currently has no advertising SDK and does not sell personal data or perform cross-app tracking. We do not currently use analytics or diagnostics services that send analytics or device-usage data to us.

Security and choices

You can edit or remove your local profile from the app or delete the app to remove its on-device data. We use reasonable safeguards for information handled by service providers. You can contact support with privacy questions.

Children's privacy

AstroDaily is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date.

Questions about privacy? Contact support.
